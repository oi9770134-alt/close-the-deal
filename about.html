How it works

1

Plan

Pick a character and their shop. Set your price and offer, buy stock, and decide where to advertise.

2

Talk to customers

Customers walk into your shop and speak to you. Answer their worries. Watch their patience: if it falls, you are losing them.

3

See what you really made

After 3 weeks you get a report: what earned you money, what cost you, and a plan for real life. Share your result and your certificate.

Every customer has their own mood and patience. Reply well and they buy. Reply badly and they walk out.

Grow your shop. Win more customers to unlock decorations and levels.

Built to play on a phone

It opens in your browser. Nothing to install. People walk, talk and gesture in 3D, and customers speak their lines out loud.

What you will learn

  • Revenue is not profit. See what is left after costs.
  • Answer the real worry (trust, delivery, price) before you give a discount.
  • Follow up. Many sales are won on the second message.
  • Cash, stock and profit are three different things.
  • Adverts only pay if you can reply fast.
